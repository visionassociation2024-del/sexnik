Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Privacy-First Commitment

Your privacy is of utmost importance to us. niksex does not require user registration, account creation, email signups, or real-name identification to browse and watch video streams.

2. LocalStorage & Client-Side Personalization

Our smart recommendations and "Watch History" feature store data strictly inside your local browser memory (HTML5 LocalStorage). This data is never sent to or recorded on our central servers, and you can erase your local history at any time using the "Clear History" button on the website.

3. Log Data & Serverless Telemetry

Like most web platforms, our serverless hosting infrastructure may collect standard, non-personally identifiable technical access logs (such as browser type, operating system, and request timestamps) strictly for performance optimization and DDoS mitigation.

4. Third-Party Content & Cookies

When you play embedded video streams, third-party hosting providers may set their own technical cookies to deliver media packets. We recommend reviewing the privacy statements of respective hosting providers.
