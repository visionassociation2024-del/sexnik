Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

1. Information We Collect

We do not require user account registration for browsing. Non-personally identifiable technical telemetry (e.g. browser type, anonymized IP country code, device viewport) is processed strictly for performance optimization and CDN caching.

2. Local Storage & Pin Boards

Your "Saved Pins" and "Watch History" are stored entirely within your local browser storage (HTML5 LocalStorage). This data is private to your device and never uploaded to our central databases.

3. Cookies & Advertising Partners

Third-party advertising networks may utilize anonymized cookies to serve contextual advertisements. You may configure your browser to reject cookies or utilize private browsing modes at any time.
